\subsection*{Contributors}

This work was supervised by a dissertation committee co-chaired by Professor Ergun Akleman of Visual Computing \& Computational Media and Professor Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy of Mechanical Engineering, Texas A\&M University, with committee members \tamumemberone, \tamumembertwo, and \tamumemberthree{} of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

The completed work is drawn from jointly authored papers, for which the student was responsible for formulation, implementation, fabrication, and writing unless noted otherwise. Co-authors were Ergun Akleman, Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy, and Matthew Ebert (Chapter~\ref{cha:modular-planar}); Alice C. Niemeyer, who contributed the group-theoretic proofs, Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy, Ergun Akleman, and, for the exact enumeration, Reymond Akpanya (Chapter~\ref{cha:volumetric-bravais}); Ergun Akleman (Chapters~\ref{cha:nonwoven} and~\ref{cha:linked}); U\u{g}ur \"Onal, Alison Martin, Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy, and Ergun Akleman (Chapter~\ref{cha:hgrs}); and Himani Deshpande, Madelein Olivan Villegas, Ergun Akleman, and Jeeeun Kim (optical channels, Chapter~\ref{cha:manufacturing}).

The synthesis and the proposed work are the student's own, under the guidance of the co-chairs.
