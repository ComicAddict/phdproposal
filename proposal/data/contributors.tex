\subsection*{Contributors}

This proposal was supervised by a dissertation committee co-chaired by Professor Ergun Akleman of Visual Computing \& Computation Media and Professor Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy of the Mechanical Engineering, Texas A\&M University, with additional committee members \tamumemberone, \tamumembertwo~and~\tamumemberthree~of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

The research summarized in this proposal is drawn from a set of jointly authored manuscripts. Unless otherwise noted, the student was responsible for the primary implementation, design exploration, and manuscript preparation for each of these works, under the direction of the committee co-chairs. Specific contributions are as follows:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Modular planar weave tiles} (Chapter~III): Co-authored with Ergun Akleman, Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy, and Matthew Ebert.
\item \textbf{Volumetric Bravais weaves and cP-weave classification} (Chapter~IV): Co-authored with Alice C. Niemeyer, Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy, and Ergun Akleman. Prof.\ Niemeyer provided the formal group-theoretic proofs and definitions underlying the cP-weave classification and its uniqueness results; Prof.\ Krishnamurthy identified the $\mathrm{GF}(2)^3$ fundamental-group structure and its operations; Prof.\ Akleman conceived the initial formalism. The enumeration of partial cubic and hexagonal primitive weaves was carried out jointly with Reymond Akpanya and Alice C. Niemeyer.
\item \textbf{Algebraic framework for volumetric nonwoven textiles} (Chapter~V): Co-authored with Ergun Akleman.
\item \textbf{Algebraic design calculus, hypergraph rotation systems, and primal--dual polycatenanes} (Chapter~VI): Co-authored with Ergun Akleman and Vinayak R. Krishnamurthy, and, for the polycatenane construction, additional collaborators on that manuscript.
\item \textbf{Optical pathway fabrication} (referenced briefly in Chapter~I as related, ancillary work): Co-authored with Himani Deshpande, Madelein Olivan Villegas, Ergun Akleman, and Jeeeun Kim; the student's contribution was to the fabrication and design of the woven/channel test geometries.
\end{itemize}

All other work conducted in preparation of this proposal, including the synthesis of these results into a unified framework and the design of the proposed manufacturing and mechanical-characterization studies in Chapter~VII, was completed by the student independently, under the guidance of the committee co-chairs.
